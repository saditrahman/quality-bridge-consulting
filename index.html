Who We Are

A Professional Advisory Firm Built for Bangladesh

Quality Bridge Consulting is dedicated to advancing food safety, industrial hygiene, agribusiness growth, and sustainable development. We combine global best practices with deep local expertise to help companies, SMEs, and development partners comply with regulations, strengthen operations, and unlock new market opportunities.

Our team brings together specialists in food science, pharmaceuticals, agribusiness, industrial safety, environment, and market systems — delivering practical, results-driven solutions.
